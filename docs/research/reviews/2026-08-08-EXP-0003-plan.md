# EXP-0003 planning review

Status: non-authoritative research memo
Stage: plan
Date: 2026-08-08

## Hypothesis

Bounded Experience can improve both latency and estimated energy over cold
selection on honest Apple Silicon holdouts without unacceptable negative
transfer or full-history quality loss.

## Evidence inventory

- analytical ToyDaphnis history in EXP-0001;
- implemented native C checksum harness and versioned Gate 1 schemas;
- committed 24-holdout fixed-C manifest;
- no EXP-0003 silicon measurement, sealed run, remote verification, TVM result,
  or independent reproduction.

## Data quality

The manifest fixes warm-up, repetition, random seed, candidate set, shapes,
compiler flags, evidence class, and thermal fail-closed policy. Power sampling
resolution and privilege behavior remain untested in a real run.

## Problems

Policy selections must be generated without target-oracle leakage. Short
workloads may not provide enough powermetrics samples. Google Drive behavior is
unit-tested through command boundaries only, not against a real remote.

## Results and non-claims

There is no performance result. C checksum agreement is a semantic harness
fact, not evidence that Experience improves performance or energy. No RISC-V,
Daphnis, QEMU-hardware, FPGA, or cross-Mac claim is available.

## Implications and counterevidence

Fixed C isolates the measurement contract before TVM variability. It may still
be a poor predictor of TVM transfer; a disagreement is counterevidence and must
keep the Gate open.

## Issue candidates and next goals

Use existing T-0022/T-0024/T-0025 for retention/policy outcomes, reporting, and
failed variants. Validate T-0065 against the real remote during the pilot; do
not allocate another issue until a distinct actionable defect is observed.

## Recommendation

`continue`: run a small authorized powermetrics pilot after merge, inspect
sample duration/noise, then decide whether to increase inner iterations before
the full fixed-C dataset. Pivot or pause if energy sampling cannot meet the
registered contract.
