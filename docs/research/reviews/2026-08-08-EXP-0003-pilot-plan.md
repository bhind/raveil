# EXP-0003 powermetrics pilot-plan review

Status: Non-authoritative research memo
Stage: Pilot planning
Date: 2026-08-08

## Hypothesis

Native-C measurement windows calibrated to roughly 400 ms for the fastest
observed candidate will yield at least three powermetrics CPU-power samples
under a stable nominal thermal state. This pilot tests sampling adequacy only;
it cannot support the Gate 1 latency or energy hypothesis.

## Evidence inventory

- committed native-C checksum harness and candidate families;
- one-iteration host calibration across the 24-workload full manifest;
- observed invocation range of approximately 18 to 487 microseconds;
- 100 ms configured powermetrics sampling interval;
- rclone 1.75.0 and repository-external `gdrive:` OAuth configuration;
- failed RUN-ID `20260808T112420Z-af35dd5c0-d6179399`, its sealed bundle, and
  successful remote hash/size verification.

## Data quality

The timing calibration was short, unsealed, and collected outside the formal
run lifecycle. It is suitable for choosing conservative pilot window lengths,
not for comparing candidates. The first formal run contains only two records
and is incomplete; its partial measurements are useful for sampler-boundary
diagnosis, not candidate comparison.

## Problems

- Original inner-iteration counts made many measured windows shorter than one
  power-sampling interval.
- Sudo authentication is terminal/session specific and must precede the run in
  the same user-operated terminal.
- A new powermetrics process could start more slowly than a fast candidate;
  sequence 2 completed before the sampler emitted any observation.

## Results and non-claims

The manifests now separate a six-workload/five-repetition pilot from the
24-holdout/15-repetition full experiment. Workload-specific inner iterations
target roughly 400 ms and actual measurements require three power samples. The
first formal RUN-ID failed after two semantically valid records: sequence 1 had
nine samples, while sequence 2 had zero. The failed bundle is sealed and
remotely verified. There is no complete latency result, energy result, policy
result, or Gate conclusion.

## Implications and possible refutations

The zero-byte second raw file refuted the assumption that calibrated duration
alone guarantees sampler availability. A readiness observation must precede
each benchmark and remain excluded from its aggregate. If the corrected pilot
still returns fewer than three window samples or changes thermal state, the
window calibration must increase before the full run.
Even a successful pilot does not show that Experience improves either metric.
The same-Mac CPU-power estimate cannot support RISC-V, QEMU, Daphnis, FPGA, or
other-Mac performance claims.
