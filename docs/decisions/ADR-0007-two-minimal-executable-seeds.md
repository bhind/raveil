# ADR-0007: Bootstrap with two minimal executable seeds

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-07

## Context

The broad architecture and historical hybrid prototype were outrunning
executable evidence.

## Decision

Reset to `v0.0000000000001` with two independent tracks:

1. a freestanding RV64 Sonatine authority/shell seed;
2. a host Python bounded-Experience loop using analytical ToyDaphnis.

Do not add LLMs, production RTL, broad framework integration, or accelerator
claims until the corresponding gates justify them.

## Consequences

The two tracks are deliberately not integrated. Historical prototypes remain
design evidence, not current implementation. Small reproducible gates take
precedence over wholesale restoration of a former prototype.
