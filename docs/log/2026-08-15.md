## ADR-0048 EXP-0008 evidence-promotion boundary

- Accepted ADR-0048 under existing T-0044. EXP-0008 already records the frozen
  manifests, failed and recovery RUN-IDs, exact commands, all-candidate
  256-input oracle result, nested 1/4/16/64/256 accounts, identity hashes,
  derived-report SHA-256, and raw-seal SHA-256. No new measurement or
  performance interpretation was introduced.
- The remaining reproducibility gap is durability: the generated raw bundles
  are ignored and currently machine-local, with no recorded immutable remote
  copy or download-based check. ADR-0048 applies ADR-0009's immutable-copy,
  marker-last, readback, and secret-exclusion rules to both the retained failed
  RUN and completed recovery RUN before external promotion.
- Added `docs/guides/T-0044-EXP-0008-EVIDENCE-PROMOTION.md` as the Jitro owner
  packet. It forbids simulator reruns and sealed-byte changes, requires exact
  local seal verification, immutable remote transfer, download verification,
  completion-marker readback, a small non-sensitive tracked receipt, mutation
  tests, and fail-closed handling when credentials or source evidence are
  unavailable.
- This is a documentation/decision change. EXP-0008 remains
  `advance-partial-latency-traffic`; T-0044, RFC-0005 go, energy, timing, area,
  IP disposition, other Graph organizations, FPGA, ASIC, and silicon remain
  open or non-claims.
