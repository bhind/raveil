## T-0022 bounded-memory preregistration correction

- The first target policy-plan dry run produced a complete 144-row matrix but
  exposed that `active_memory_limit: 256` retained all 240 source summaries.
  Bounded, FIFO, reservoir, and random therefore could not exercise their
  retention boundaries.
- Before collecting any target measurement, source-only simulation compared
  limits 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 192, and 256. The target manifest now fixes
  the limit at 64, a 4x reduction from full history. At that limit bounded,
  FIFO, reservoir, and random differed from full history on 5, 2, 12, and 24
  of 24 target slates respectively.
- This is a preregistration correction, not target evidence or a Gate result.
  The plan generated under the old manifest hash is invalid and will not be
  used for measurement.
