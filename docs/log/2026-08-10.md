## T-0015 blocking IPC and leaf delegation

- Kept the bounded four-message endpoint queue and added explicit
  `OK/BLOCKED/DENIED/INVALID` results. Empty receives and full sends record an
  operation-specific task wait; the opposite successful operation wakes the
  lowest-ID matching waiter, and the caller retries without retained pointers.
- Accepted ADR-0015: only a source owner holding `CONTROL` may create a
  nonempty, rights-attenuated leaf grant. Delegated grants cannot carry
  `CONTROL`; source revocation does not cascade and remains a documented future
  design boundary.
- Host coverage exercises blocking/wake-up, ready-only selection, delegated
  send/receive, authority isolation, escalation rejection, and invalid endpoint
  failure. QEMU evidence remains emulation correctness, not scheduling
  performance or persistent multi-user execution evidence.

## T-0016 fault containment boundaries

  and unknown syscalls now record a diagnostic and take the kernel-return path
