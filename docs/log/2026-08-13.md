## T-0042 commit-aware semantic attribution decision

- ADR-0045 accepts a CPU-owned, non-software-writable token and epoch as the
  next semantic-attribution candidate. It explicitly rejects TileLink source,
  DCache origin, software phase, PC alone, and A/D completion alone as semantic
  identity.
- Pinned-source inspection located Rocket MEM/WB, DCache kill/replay/exception,
  and retirement signals, plus BOOM LSU request states, ROB/load/store queue
  identity, replay/kill/exception, and commit signals. This is read-only design
  evidence, not an implemented probe or functional result.
- Replay retains one token across distinct core and DCache attempts; token or
  epoch exhaustion and alias fail closed. Kill, exception, rollback, reset,
  stale epoch, or a duplicate outcome also fails closed. Loads require
  successful response plus retirement, while stores require retirement,
  CPU-specific store authorization, and actual owned-manager D completion.
  Loader/FESVR/Debug/untagged traffic cannot mint CPU tokens. A post-A exception
  cannot cancel transport, so any later side effect is recorded as an explicit
  lifecycle violation rather than attributed commit.
- Rocket and BOOM diagnostics, their positive/negative lifecycle stimuli, and
  connection to the ADR-0043 bridge remain future work. No EXP was added, and
